Art direction · concept v5

Gloss
without blocks

The site as a glossy magazine spread. No cards or panels: photos of Alla's work run edge to edge, huge words cross the images, colour fields span the full width. The first screen holds a living paint stroke in the colours of her hair.

01 · Idea

Work instead of decoration. The site's main graphics are now photos from Alla's portfolio: studio shoots, competition looks, curls. They sit large and frameless, and a glossy glint passes over them on hover.

Layers like a magazine. The word “Colour” sits over three photos, “Curls” slips behind a photo, and in services a photo of the work follows the cursor. Sections are separated by colour and air, not boxes.

02 · Colour

Rose gold#FFD9E6 → #E7D6FFField of the “Curls” section

03 · Motion

  • The paint stroke flows and shimmers (WebGL), and draws itself left to right on load.
  • Photos open with a bottom-up wipe, and gallery columns drift at different speeds.
  • In services a photo of the work follows the cursor; on phones there is a thumbnail in the row instead.
  • A glossy glint over photos on hover. With reduced motion everything is static.

04 · To confirm

  • Awards and dates are taken from Instagram posts: check the wording.
  • Figures, address, hours, phone and brands light up with the “Highlight placeholders” button.
  • Consent from photographers and models to use the images on the site, with credits naming them.
